Our Story

A hidden treasure
in the heart of Nairobi

Ora Café was born from a simple idea: that a meal should be more than fuel — it should be a moment. Tucked along Waiyaki Way, we've created a space where Japandi minimalism meets Nairobi warmth, where the food is as considered as the space you eat it in.

Every dish is crafted with intention. Every corner designed with care. Whether you're here for a slow morning coffee or a long lunch with people you love, Ora is your pause in the city.

Good to Know

Before you
visit us

What are your opening hours? +
We're open every day and close at 10 PM. We recommend arriving by 9:30 PM for a relaxed last order.
Is your menu halal? +
Yes — Ora Café is 100% halal. Every item on our menu meets halal standards.
Do you have outdoor seating? +
Yes, we have a shaded patio for outdoor dining — perfect for Nairobi's sunny afternoons.
Is there Wi-Fi? +
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for all guests. Ask our team for the password when you arrive.
Do you have a kids' menu? +
Yes! We have a kids' menu with lighter, family-friendly options. Ora is welcoming to all ages.
Do I need to book in advance? +
Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend reserving ahead — especially on weekends — to guarantee your preferred spot.
Where exactly are you located? +
We're on Waiyaki Way, Nairobi. Use the map below or open in Google Maps for directions.
